# Version History for Shantay

## v0.2.0 (April ??, 2025)

Shantay has matured into being a complete tool now. It supports two different
workflows for exploring the data, one that is centered on a subset of the data
and runs in minutes—tasks *prepare*, *analyze*, and *visualize*—and one that
considers all statements of reasons and runs in hours—tasks *summarize* and
*visualize*. Their implementation is shared and builds on declarative schemas to
determine the layout of the source data, the computation of summary statistics,
and the visualization of said statistics through graphs.

## v0.1.0 (March 1, 2025)

Initial release that barely makes for a working prototype.
